This guide walks you through a complete P2P BTC/fiat trade using the `@satsails/p2p-trading-sdk`. You'll act as both seller and buyer to understand the full flow — in production, each party uses their own client instance. Sails Protocol never takes custody of funds at any point: every lock, transfer, and release happens directly between you and your counterparty.

<Note>
  In production, seller and buyer run in separate processes and separate wallets — they share only `trade.id` and `escrow.id` to coordinate. Neither party has access to the other's `SailsClient` instance or session token.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The default escrow type is `MOCK` on a local development node. For production non-custodial flows over real Bitcoin — using on-chain multisig (`MULTISIG`) or Lightning HODL invoices (`LIGHTNING_HODL`) — see the [Escrow Lifecycle](/guides/escrow-lifecycle) guide.
</Tip>
