Sails Protocol is open infrastructure for building interoperable **P2P financial marketplaces**. It standardizes the identity, liquidity discovery, trade coordination, escrow, dispute, and reputation layers that every wallet used to rebuild from scratch. Integrate through the **Sails P2P Trading SDK** and let your users trade directly with each other.

## How to integrate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the SDK">
    Add `@satsails/p2p-trading-sdk` (and `@satsails/sdk-react` for React apps). One `SailsClient` gives you every module.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register an identity">
    Create an Ed25519 identity — the SDK proves possession of the key and signs in with a challenge. No email or password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or discover offers">
    Publish an offer (asset, price, payment method) to the node's offer book, or search it for offers from other participants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade and settle">
    Open a trade, agree on details in the trade channel, lock the asset in escrow, and release it once the off-chain payment arrives. If something goes wrong, an arbiter's signed ruling settles the dispute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes Sails different

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="No central custodian" icon="shield" href="/concepts/settlement">
    Client-held-key escrows need two of three keys to move funds — no single party, including the node, can move them alone.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Many assets and rails" icon="link" href="/guides/escrow-lifecycle">
    BTC, Lightning, EVM stablecoins, and more behind one settlement interface, with each provider's custody model disclosed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI-assisted, human-approved" icon="robot" href="/guides/ai-agents">
    Local AI (QVAC) drafts intents and flags risk. It never signs or moves funds.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
