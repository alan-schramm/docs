Sails Protocol is open infrastructure for building interoperable P2P Financial Marketplaces. It standardizes the identity, reputation, escrow, settlement, and discovery infrastructure that every wallet used to rebuild from scratch — so you can integrate a fully non-custodial P2P marketplace in a single SDK call. Whether you're a Bitcoin wallet, a multi-chain fintech, an OTC desk, or an AI trading agent, Sails gives you the coordination layer without ever touching your users' funds.

## How to integrate Sails Protocol

These four steps take you from zero to a working P2P trade. Each step links to a dedicated guide with full details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the SDK">
    Add `@satsails/p2p-trading-sdk` to your project with npm, yarn, or pnpm. The SDK wraps every protocol module — identity, liquidity, trading, settlement, and reputation — behind a single `SailsClient` interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your identity">
    Create a cryptographic Ed25519 identity for your application or your user. This identity is portable across the entire Sails network and accumulates reputation over time — no email, no password, no centralized account required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish or discover offers">
    Publish a liquidity offer (asset, price, payment method) to the P2P network, or query the order book to discover existing offers from other participants. Discovery runs over HyperDHT — no central server to query.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute a trade">
    Open a trade against an offer, negotiate terms over an end-to-end encrypted chat channel, lock funds in a non-custodial escrow, and release them once the off-chain payment is confirmed. Sails coordinates every step — your keys never leave your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>
